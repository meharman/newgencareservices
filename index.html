About New Gen Care

Supporting independence, dignity and quality of life.

NewGen Care Group Inc. provides a comprehensive range of home care services designed to support individuals in the comfort and familiarity of their own homes.


Our services include non-medical personal support and companion care, as well as selected medical services delivered by qualified healthcare professionals where permitted by applicable provincial regulations.


We believe that quality care begins with understanding each person's unique needs. Our goal is to provide respectful, dependable and person-centred support that promotes independence, dignity and peace of mind.
