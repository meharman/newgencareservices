About New Gen Care

People first. Care delivered with purpose.

New Gen Care Group Inc. provides dependable home care and support services designed around each client's individual needs.

Our goal is to help people remain comfortable, independent and supported in their own homes while giving families confidence and peace of mind.

Services range from companionship and personal care to dementia support, respite care, recovery assistance and selected healthcare services delivered by appropriately qualified professionals.
